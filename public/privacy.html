Privacy Policy: Monarchi Hub

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Mon'Archi (monarchi.agency) runs "Monarchi Hub", an internal tool used only by our own team.

What the app does

It reads advertising performance data (campaigns, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) and account names and IDs from Google Ads accounts that our team manages, through the Google Ads API, in order to prepare internal and client reports.

Data we access

Authorisation to read Google Ads data (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) granted by an authorised member of our team, and the advertising data of the accounts they manage. We do not collect personal data of people who see the ads.

How we use it

Only for reporting, analysis and optimisation of those same accounts. We do not sell or rent the data and we do not use it for advertising. Data may be processed by our AI service provider (Anthropic) solely to generate analyses for our team.

Storage and security

Data is stored in our database, accessible only to signed-in team members. Access tokens are kept on our server and never in the browser.

Retention and removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete stored data, contact us.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
